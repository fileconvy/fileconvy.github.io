Which file tool do I need?

A one-page cheat sheet for everyday file jobs: what to use, and the one mistake that usually goes wrong. Every tool linked here is free on FileConvy, with no signup and no watermark.

PDF jobs

I want to…UseWatch out for
Edit the text of a PDFPDF to WordScanned PDFs are pictures of text; run OCR first.
Send a document that looks the same everywhereWord to PDF or Excel to PDFSet the print area in Excel, or wide sheets split across pages.
Get a PDF under an email or upload limitCompress PDFCompress once from the original; re-compressing blurs images.
Put several files into one PDFMerge PDFCheck the page order before you download.
Send only a few pagesSplit PDFPage numbers printed on the pages may differ from PDF page numbers.
Work with a table from a PDF statementPDF to ExcelCheck merged cells and totals after converting.
Turn a PDF into slidesPDF to PowerPointEach page becomes one slide.
Use a PDF page as an imagePDF to JPG or PDF to PNGPNG for text and diagrams, JPG for photos.

Image jobs

I want to…UseWatch out for
Make a photo smaller for a form or websiteCompress Image then Resize ImageForms often limit both KB and pixel size; check both.
Open an iPhone photo on WindowsHEIC to JPGOr set the iPhone camera to "Most Compatible".
Cut out a product or personRemove BackgroundSave as PNG to keep the transparency.
Copy text from a screenshot or photoImage to Text (OCR)Straight, well-lit photos give far better results.
Make a passport or visa photoPassport Photo MakerPlain light background, no shadows, neutral face.
Use a logo in a cutting machine or large printPNG to SVGWorks best on simple, high-contrast artwork.
Make images load faster on a websiteWebP ConverterKeep the original in case you need to edit later.

Text and developer jobs

I want to…UseWatch out for
Find what changed between two versionsText DiffTrailing spaces show as changes.
Read or fix messy JSONJSON FormatterJSON needs double quotes and no trailing commas.
Open JSON data in a spreadsheetJSON to CSVNested objects are not flattened into columns; flatten them first.
See what is inside a login tokenJWT DecoderDecoding is not verifying; never trust an unverified token.
Schedule a job at the right timeCron Expression GeneratorServers usually run cron in UTC.
Test a pattern before putting it in codeRegex TesterRegex flavours differ slightly between languages.

Paperwork

Need an invoice, a rental agreement, an HR letter or an Excel cash book rather than a converter? The templates library has 82 free Word and Excel templates: CVs, invoices, agreements, HR letters and accounting sheets. This guide explains which ones each type of business needs.

Before you upload anything

Most FileConvy tools upload your file over HTTPS to process it and delete the temporary copy after the result is returned. A few (HEIC to JPG, Video to MP3, Image Upscaler and developer tools such as the JWT decoder and hash generator) run entirely in your browser. For very sensitive documents, remove what you don't need to share first.
